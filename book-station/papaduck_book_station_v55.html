v55 (2026-10-12): 🛡️ FAMILY CHECK before Send to Drafts. The same call that identifies the book now also asks whether a Christian homeschool parent would be surprised by it (nudity, sexual content, graphic violence, heavy profanity, occult or horror focus, drug glorification, crude humor). Type the number from the sticker you put on the book, and that is the PD number, full stop. The Station picks the pool from the number itself (under 500 = batch, 5000+ = CD/record, otherwise single), jumps that counter to your sticker, and ➕ New Book carries on from there. Quantity no longer switches the pool or blocks Upload — 3, 10 or 50 copies all keep your sticker number. It only turns green once the book has actually reached Drafts. And ➕ New Book now asks before moving on if the book in front of you was never sent. Every book in 🗂️ Saved Books also has a 📤 Send to Drafts button, and the old text is still there as a backup (📋 Copy text). Tick it and Claude is told, in the paste block, to leave the photos alone — no blur, no pixelate, no holding the book back, and no asking you about it again — while still disclosing in Block 1 and Block 2 that a previous owner's name is present and never printing the name anywhere: not the eBay title, not either block, not the AbeBooks description, not the Airtable Notes. From v44: 📕 ABEBOOKS ONLY and 🏷️ EBAY ONLY tiles — sitting right beside the Amazon tile and working exactly the same way. Until now the only channel choice on this screen was amazon, which has always meant Amazon-only. Now there are three, and they are mutually exclusive — clicking one releases the other two. 📕 AbeBooks only tells Claude to build the AbeBooks flat-file row and no eBay CSV row, and to set Channel Assignment to AbeBooks so a later session cannot sweep it into an eBay upload. 🏷️ eBay only does the reverse — eBay row, no AbeBooks listing and no registry entry. Each tile has its own price box, same as Amazon: type what you want the book listed at and Claude uses that number as-is, still runs the market check, and still tells you if the comps disagree — but never changes your number on its own. Leave a price blank and it researches and recommends one the normal way. Pick nothing and the book behaves as it always has: eBay and AbeBooks. The choice is recorded in the paste block and shows as a badge in Saved Books, so re-copying an old book keeps its channel. Note AbeBooks has no Best Offer, so an AbeBooks-only book skips the Min Offer / Auto-Accept bands entirely. From v43: 🗂️ SAVED BOOKS NOW HOLDS 1,000, AND NOTHING IS EVER THROWN AWAY AGAIN. Until v42 this list kept the newest 200 books and silently deleted everything older — no warning, no copy, gone. The cap is now 1,000, a live counter sits at the top of the list, and when you come within 10 of the limit (990) a banner appears asking you to archive. 📦 Archive oldest writes those books to a real .json file in your Downloads folder and clears them off the list, so the list stays fast and your history stays complete. 📂 Open an archive file loads any archive straight back in and every book in it still has its 📋 Copy for Claude button, so you can always go back. A log of every archive you have made — file name, how many books, which numbers, when — stays on screen underneath. And if this computer ever runs out of browser storage mid-book, the Station now archives the oldest 300 to a file automatically and saves your book anyway instead of losing it. Before v42 the pool was a sticky manual choice: pick 📦 Batch lot once and every book after it kept drawing from the ≤500 counter until you noticed and clicked back. On 2026-09-08 that quietly spent four batch numbers on single books (PD-0113, PD-0115, PD-0117, PD-0118) — nothing errored, and those numbers are gone for good because a PD number belongs to its book for life. The 💿 CD / Record pool is never auto-switched; discs keep their own counter whatever the quantity. Also new in v42: the book-identification prompt stops inventing dust jackets. It is now told that a glossy printed cover is a laminated pictorial hardcover and NOT a jacket, that a slipcase is a slipcase, that it must see actual folded flaps before claiming a jacket at all — and that glare is not damage.
